# Abilities

## Summary

Abilities fall into three categories:

Basic Attacks
:   Relatively simple abilities that each cat has exactly one of. They cost no mana to use and can be buffed or replaced by a number of different effects (including all Collars—see below). Can only be used once per turn normally, but can be refreshed in a number of ways.

Active Abilities, also known as Spells
:   Actions that cost 0 or more [![Mana](images/Stat_Mana.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Mana "Mana") [Mana](/wiki/Stats#Mana "Stats") to use and can be used any number of times per turn (unless otherwise stated). A cat cannot learn more than 4 spells and will be prompted to replace one when trying to learn a 5th.

    Starter Abilities
    :   12 classes receive a random spell when starting an [adventure](/wiki/Adventure "Adventure") from a pool of 10 starter spells. [![Collarless Icon.svg](images/Collarless_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Collarless "Collarless") [Collarless](/wiki/Collarless "Collarless") can receive any of the 108 spells it can receive on level up as a starter spell. [![Jester Icon.svg](images/Jester_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Jester "Jester") [Jester](/wiki/Jester "Jester") may receive any non-Collarless spell as their starter spell.

    [Bonus Abilities](/wiki/Bonus_Abilities "Bonus Abilities")
    :   Abilities that exclusively occupy a bonus spell slot that can be granted by various Actives, Passives & Items. Some Bonus Abilities are unique and cannot be learned as regular spells, such as [![ABILITY Seppuku.svg](images/ABILITY_Seppuku.svg)](/wiki/Seppuku "Seppuku") [Seppuku](/wiki/Seppuku "Seppuku") [![ABILITY Seppuku.svg](images/ABILITY_Seppuku.svg)](/wiki/Seppuku "Seppuku")  ![Necromancer](images/Necromancer_Icon.png "Necromancer") [Seppuku](/wiki/Seppuku "Seppuku")  Down yourself. . A cat or familiar can only have 1 Bonus Ability and they will overwrite each other contextually.

    [Replacement Abilities](/wiki/Replacement_Abilities "Replacement Abilities")
    :   Abilities which replace a spell or a cat's basic attack such as [![ABILITY Reposition.svg](images/ABILITY_Reposition.svg)](/wiki/Reposition "Reposition") [Reposition](/wiki/Reposition "Reposition") [![ABILITY Reposition.svg](images/ABILITY_Reposition.svg)](/wiki/Reposition "Reposition")  ![Fighter](images/Fighter_Icon.png "Fighter") [Reposition](/wiki/Reposition "Reposition")  Move up to two tiles. , [![ABILITY Wolf Claws.svg](images/ABILITY_Wolf_Claws.svg)](/wiki/Wolf_Claws "Wolf Claws") [Wolf Claws](/wiki/Wolf_Claws "Wolf Claws") [![ABILITY Wolf Claws.svg](images/ABILITY_Wolf_Claws.svg)](/wiki/Wolf_Claws "Wolf Claws")  ![Druid](images/Druid_Icon.png "Druid") [Wolf Claws](/wiki/Wolf_Claws "Wolf Claws")  A 2-hit melee attack. , and ![ITEM Crown of Pestilence.svg](images/ITEM_Crown_of_Pestilence.svg) [Crown of Pestilence](/wiki/Crown_of_Pestilence "Crown of Pestilence") [![ITEM Crown of Pestilence.svg](images/ITEM_Crown_of_Pestilence.svg)](/wiki/Crown_of_Pestilence "Crown of Pestilence")  [Crown of Pestilence](/wiki/Crown_of_Pestilence "Crown of Pestilence") (Head)  Your basic attack becomes Pestilence. .

    Familiar Abilities
    :   Abilities unique to familiars such as [![ABILITY Aeroblast.svg](images/ABILITY_Aeroblast.svg)](/wiki/Aeroblast "Aeroblast") [Aeroblast](/wiki/Aeroblast "Aeroblast") [![ABILITY Aeroblast.svg](images/ABILITY_Aeroblast.svg)](/wiki/Aeroblast "Aeroblast")  ![Druid](images/Druid_Icon.png "Druid") [Aeroblast](/wiki/Aeroblast "Aeroblast")  A wind gust that deals 1 damage and 1 Knockback in a line. .

Passive Abilities
:   Passive effects that are or can always be in effect. A cat cannot learn more than 2 passive abilities and will be prompted to replace one when trying to learn a 3rd.  
    Unlike Actives, a class can start with any of their Passives, instead of small pool.

[Disorders](/wiki/Disorders "Disorders")
:   Functionally similar to Passive Abilities in that they apply a persistent passive effect, with usually both a negative and positive effect. They do not contribute to the passive ability cap. A cat cannot have more than 2 disorders at a time.

### Categories

Basic Attacks, Active Abilities, and Bonus Abilities have several different archetypes.

- ![Active Type Melee Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Melee_Icon.svg) Melee abilities do [![UI Physical Damage Icon.svg](images/UI_Physical_Damage_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Physical_Damage "Physical Damage") Physical Damage, typically to adjacent units, and generally scale off of [![Strength](images/Stat_Strength.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Strength") [Strength](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Stats"). They use Reach-increases to target things from farther away.
  - Melee abilities will trigger "on-contact" effects, such as damage from thorns. However, using reach to attack from a non-adjacent tile will not trigger these effects.
- ![Active Type Ranged Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Ranged_Icon.svg) Ranged abilities also do [![UI Physical Damage Icon.svg](images/UI_Physical_Damage_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Physical_Damage "Physical Damage") Physical Damage but generally scale off of [![Dexterity](images/Stat_Dexterity.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Dexterity "Dexterity") [Dexterity](/wiki/Stats#Dexterity "Stats"). They use Range-increases to target things from farther away.
  - Ranged abilities will never trigger on-contact effects, even while adjacent to an enemy.
- ![Active Type Magic Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Magic_Icon.svg) Magic abilities typically do [![UI Magic Damage Icon.svg](images/UI_Magic_Damage_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Magic_Damage "Magic Damage") Magic Damage. Compared to Melee and Ranged, much more of these have no stat-scaling at all.
- ![Active Type Heal Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Heal_Icon.svg) Heal abilities [![UI Heal Icon.svg](images/UI_Heal_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Heal "Heal") Heal units or remove [Debuffs](/wiki/Debuffs "Debuffs"). Some of these abilities can also Revive units.
- ![Active Type Defense Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Defense_Icon.svg) Defense abilities are of defensive utility: some grant [![UI Shield Icon.svg](images/UI_Shield_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Shield "Shield") Shield, [![UI Holy Shield Icon.svg](images/UI_Holy_Shield_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Holy_Shield "Holy Shield") Holy Shield, or defensive [Buffs](/wiki/Buffs "Buffs"); some make obstacles; and some push foes away.
- ![Active Type Movement Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Movement_Icon.svg) Movement abilities move the user. The category has some overlap with others, but movement is the primary objective of these abilities.
- ![Active Type Buff Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Buff_Icon.svg) Buff abilities generally give [Buffs](/wiki/Buffs "Buffs") (that aren't strictly defensive) without any direct [![UI Heal Icon.svg](images/UI_Heal_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Heal "Heal") Healing.
- ![Active Type Debuff Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Debuff_Icon.svg) Debuff abilities generally inflict [Debuffs](/wiki/Debuffs "Debuffs") without any direct damage.
- ![Active Type Spawn Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Spawn_Icon.svg) Spawn abilities generate entities: some make [Familiars](/wiki/Familiars "Familiars") belonging to the user, some make [Pickups](/wiki/Pickups "Pickups"), and some make obstacles.
- ![Active Type Misc Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Misc_Icon.svg) Misc abilities generally don't fit into the other categories but fit a special role or archetype for the [Class](/wiki/Class "Class") that has them. Abilities that have an "equal" number of buffs and debuffs also go into this category.
- ![Active Type Unknown Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Unknown_Icon.svg) Unknown abilities comprise everything else. They typically have somewhat-niche use cases, or are more complex than Misc abilities.

### [Tank Icon.svg](/wiki/Tank "Tank") [Tank](/wiki/Tank "Tank")

Many Tank abilities increase a cat's survivability, help them to take damage in place of allies, or reward them for doing so.
