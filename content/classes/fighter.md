## Overview

Fighters get +2 [![Strength](images/Stat_Strength.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Strength") [Strength](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Stats") and +1 [![Speed](images/Stat_Speed.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Speed") [Speed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), but -1 [![Intelligence](images/Stat_Intelligence.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Intelligence") [Intelligence](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Stats"). When leveling up, they can gain [![Strength](images/Stat_Strength.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Strength") [Strength](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Stats"), [![Constitution](images/Stat_Constitution.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Constitution "Constitution") [Constitution](/wiki/Stats#Constitution "Stats"), or [![Speed](images/Stat_Speed.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Speed") [Speed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats").

Fighters are meant to do as much damage as possible, using their high [![Speed](images/Stat_Speed.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Speed") [Speed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats") to get to the frontlines and then hitting hard thanks to their high [![Strength](images/Stat_Strength.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Strength") [Strength](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Stats"). They primarily excel at damaging single, high-health targets, but can effectively handle groups of enemies as well. They also have numerous ways of applying [![STATUS Bruise Icon.svg](images/STATUS_Bruise_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Status_Effects#Bruise "Status Effects") [Bruise](/wiki/Status_Effects#Bruise "Status Effects"), further boosting their team's [![UI Physical Damage Icon.svg](images/UI_Physical_Damage_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Physical_Damage "Physical Damage") Physical Damage. Fighters tend to be hearty units thanks to their sufficient [![Constitution](images/Stat_Constitution.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Constitution "Constitution") [Constitution](/wiki/Stats#Constitution "Stats"), as well as occasional [![STATUS Shield Icon.svg](images/STATUS_Shield_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Keywords#Shield "Keywords") [Shield](/wiki/Keywords#Shield "Keywords") and [![STATUS Brace Icon.svg](images/STATUS_Brace_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Status_Effects#Brace "Status Effects") [Brace](/wiki/Status_Effects#Brace "Status Effects"). However, their lowered [![Intelligence](images/Stat_Intelligence.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Intelligence") [Intelligence](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Stats") causes them to regenerate less mana at the end of their turn compared to other cats, weakening their damage output during longer fights. Additionally, most Fighter abilities can only target adjacent enemies, potentially forcing Fighters into dangerous positions.

### Basic Action

A Fighter's basic action is **Melee Attack**. It is a ![Active Type Melee Icon.svg](images/Active_Type_Melee_Icon.svg) Melee attack that does [![UI Physical Damage Icon.svg](images/UI_Physical_Damage_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Physical_Damage "Physical Damage") Physical Damage to an adjacent unit equal to the user's [![Strength](images/Stat_Strength.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Strength") [Strength](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Stats").
