## Overview

Mages get +2 [![Intelligence](images/Stat_Intelligence.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Intelligence") [Intelligence](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Stats") and +2 [![Charisma](images/Stat_Charisma.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Charisma "Charisma") [Charisma](/wiki/Stats#Charisma "Stats"), but -1 [![Constitution](images/Stat_Constitution.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Constitution "Constitution") [Constitution](/wiki/Stats#Constitution "Stats") and -1 [![Strength](images/Stat_Strength.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Strength") [Strength](/wiki/Stats#Strength "Stats") as well. When leveling up, they can gain [![Intelligence](images/Stat_Intelligence.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Intelligence") [Intelligence](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Stats"), [![Charisma](images/Stat_Charisma.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Charisma "Charisma") [Charisma](/wiki/Stats#Charisma "Stats"), or [![Dexterity](images/Stat_Dexterity.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Dexterity "Dexterity") [Dexterity](/wiki/Stats#Dexterity "Stats").

Mages are usually ranged attackers with some support capabilities. They often cast more spells than other classes due to their high [![Intelligence](images/Stat_Intelligence.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Intelligence") [Intelligence](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Stats") and [![Charisma](images/Stat_Charisma.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Charisma "Charisma") [Charisma](/wiki/Stats#Charisma "Stats"). Their spells usually lack high base damage, but their offensive output can balloon with strategic use of [![UI Mana Cost Icon.svg](images/UI_Mana_Cost_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Mana "Mana") Mana, giving themselves [![STATUS Magic Damage Up Icon.svg](images/STATUS_Magic_Damage_Up_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Status_Effects#Magic_Damage_Up "Status Effects") [Magic Damage Up](/wiki/Status_Effects#Magic_Damage_Up "Status Effects"), or abusing elemental spells—of which they have a wide variety. Alternatively, these elemental spells can be used to manipulate the environment to the team's liking. However, as they have low [![Constitution](images/Stat_Constitution.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Constitution "Constitution") [Constitution](/wiki/Stats#Constitution "Stats") and few ways of improving their survivability, Mages are among the frailest classes in the game and can be downed especially easily.

### Basic Action

A Mage's basic attack is **Magic Dart**. It deals both [![UI Physical Magic Damage Icon.svg](images/UI_Physical_Magic_Damage_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Physical_Magic_Damage "Physical Magic Damage") Physical Magic Damage that scales with [![Dexterity](images/Stat_Dexterity.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Dexterity "Dexterity") [Dexterity](/wiki/Stats#Dexterity "Stats") to a unit within Range 3. It has a base damage (at 4-to-6 DEX) of 5.

Being both physical and magical, Magic Dart is affected by stacks of [![STATUS Magic Damage Up Icon.svg](images/STATUS_Magic_Damage_Up_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Status_Effects#Magic_Damage_Up "Status Effects") [Magic Damage Up](/wiki/Status_Effects#Magic_Damage_Up "Status Effects"), while still reaping the benefits of being a physical attack ([![STATUS Bruise Icon.svg](images/STATUS_Bruise_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Status_Effects#Bruise "Status Effects") [Bruise](/wiki/Status_Effects#Bruise "Status Effects"), Critical Hits, etc).

The [![ABILITY Paw Missile.svg](images/ABILITY_Paw_Missile.svg)](/wiki/Paw_Missile "Paw Missile") [Paw Missile](/wiki/Paw_Missile "Paw Missile") [![ABILITY Paw Missile.svg](images/ABILITY_Paw_Missile.svg)](/wiki/Paw_Missile "Paw Missile")  ![Mage](images/Mage_Icon.png "Mage") [Paw Missile](/wiki/Paw_Missile "Paw Missile")  Your basic attack is Magic Missile.  
(its damage scales with [![Intelligence](images/Stat_Intelligence.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Intelligence")Intelligence).  passive replaces Magic Dart with a basic attack version of **Magic Missile**, which has infinite range, ignores obstacles, and never misses. It only deals [![UI Magic Damage Icon.svg](images/UI_Magic_Damage_Icon.svg)](/wiki/Magic_Damage "Magic Damage") Magic Damage; but unlike most attacks, it scales with [![Intelligence](images/Stat_Intelligence.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Intelligence") [Intelligence](/wiki/Stats#Intelligence "Stats"), much like how most ranged abilities scale with [![Dexterity](images/Stat_Dexterity.svg)](/wiki/Stats#Dexterity "Dexterity") [Dexterity](/wiki/Stats#Dexterity "Stats"). It has a base damage (at 4-to-6 INT) of 3.
